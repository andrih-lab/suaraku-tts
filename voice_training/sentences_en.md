# Recording Sentences — English

Read these in your natural speaking voice, as if explaining something to a
friend. Pause briefly between sentences.

## Short statements
1. Good morning, how are you doing today?
2. I'm learning how to build a new application.
3. The weather is quite bright and cool today.
4. Please hand me that book on the table.
5. This coffee tastes really good and warm.
6. We're leaving at eight o'clock tomorrow morning.
7. Don't forget your umbrella, it might rain later.
8. She has been working there for five years now.
9. The house is not far from here at all.
10. Everyone has already gathered in the living room.

## Questions
11. Have you had lunch yet today?
12. When exactly is the meeting going to start?
13. How do I get to the train station from here?
14. Who is responsible for this project?
15. Why do you look so tired today?
16. How much does a ticket to the city cost?
17. What are your plans for the weekend?
18. Could you help me for just a moment?

## Exclamations & expressions
19. Wow, this view is absolutely incredible!
20. Oh no, I forgot my wallet again!
21. Congratulations! You did a fantastic job finishing that.
22. Careful, the road ahead is pretty slippery.
23. Wait, is it really this late already?
24. Amazing, the results of your work are truly impressive.

## Longer, narrative sentences
25. Every morning I like to take a walk around the neighborhood before breakfast, because the air is still fresh and it isn't too crowded yet.
26. When I was a kid, my grandfather used to tell long stories about his travels across the islands.
27. This project needs cooperation from a lot of people, from the design team to the developers and marketing.
28. Even though the trip was quite long, the view of the rice fields and mountains made the whole journey enjoyable.
29. After years of saving, our family was finally able to buy a small house on the edge of the city.
30. Artificial intelligence is advancing incredibly fast, and things that once seemed impossible are now becoming reality.

## Numbers, dates, technical terms
31. Our office phone number is zero two one, seven eight nine.
32. The meeting will be held on the twenty-third of October.
33. The total cost of this project is around fifteen thousand dollars.
34. Our server has four processor cores and four gigabytes of memory.
35. This application supports both Indonesian and English.
36. The internet speed here reaches a hundred megabits per second.

## Everyday conversation
37. Hey, how's it going? It's been a while since we last talked.
38. Thank you so much for your help, I really appreciate it.
39. Sorry I'm late, the traffic was completely jammed earlier.
40. Alright, I'll go ahead and get started on that right now.
41. It's totally fine, take your time, we still have plenty of time.
42. See you tomorrow, drive safe.
